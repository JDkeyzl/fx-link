# 03 Quality and fitment — 质量档口径与不适配免责

> 对外沟通质量档时必须说清边界；Desk 不保证互换。

## Quality tiers (outbound language)

### Genuine / OE / 原厂

- Prefer when budget allows for safety-critical or long-life applications.
- Higher cost; still verify part_no against vehicle.
- `[待确认：哪些渠道允许对外称「原厂/正品」——需与法务/采购一致]`

### OEM-quality / high-value alternative / OEM 品质替代

- Often used by maintenance fleets; disclose it is **not** claimed as OE unless documented.
- Customer must confirm fitment.

### Economy replacement / 经济型替代

- For price-sensitive markets; **clearly disclose** quality tier.
- Do not claim OE interchange without proof.

## Fitment rules

- Catalogue match on part_no ≠ guaranteed install success on every VIN/chassis variant.
- Related / substitute suggestions from tools require **customer confirmation**.
- If customer provides only Chinese name without number, treat as high risk of mismatch.

## Required disclaimer snippets

- EN: “This is a catalogue reference. Please confirm the part number against your vehicle records before ordering.”
- 中文：「以上为目录参考。下单前请对照贵司车辆档案确认件号。」
- EN: “Desk does not guarantee interchangeability of replacements.”
- 中文：「替代件是否可互换，Desk 不作保证，需您方确认。」
