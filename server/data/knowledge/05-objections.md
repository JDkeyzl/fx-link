# 05 Objections — 异议处理短话术

> 每条：场景 → 目标 → 2～3 句原话。保持短。禁止贬低竞品。

## Only wants lowest price

- Goal: clarify quality tier + collect part_no; do not race on invented discounts.
- EN: “I can show catalogue **EXW reference** by part number. If you need economy vs OE tier, tell me which you accept — sales confirms final commercial price.”
- 中文：「我可以按件号给出目录 **EXW 参考价**。若接受经济件或只要原厂，请说明档位——最终商务价由销售确认。」
- `[待确认：是否允许 Desk 提及「可申请特价」]`

## Sends competitor screenshot

- Goal: verify same part_no / same Incoterm / same inclusions.
- EN: “Please share the part number and whether their price is EXW or CIF. Catalogue reference here is EXW China only.”
- 中文：「请提供件号，并确认对方是 EXW 还是 CIF。本站目录参考仅为 EXW 中国。」

## Insists on CIF / door delivery

- Goal: keep EXW reference honest; escalate freight.
- EN: “I can lock EXW reference lines first. CIF needs cargo volume, packing and freight — our sales will calculate.”
- 中文：「可以先锁定 EXW 参考行项目。CIF 需要货量、包装与运费，由销售核算。」

## No part number, only description

- Goal: get photo / OEM number; avoid wrong shipment.
- EN: “To avoid wrong parts, please send the old part photo or OEM number. I will not guess a part number.”
- 中文：「为避免错件，请发旧件照片或原厂件号。我不会猜测件号。」

## Wants stock / ship tomorrow

- Goal: light-asset honesty.
- EN: “We operate a sourcing model; lead time is confirmed by sales after availability check — not promised in chat.”
- 中文：「我们是供应链寻源模式；交期需销售核实可供后确认，聊天中不作承诺。」
- `[待确认：是否有少量常备件可承诺更快——有则写明品类]`

## Asks for company strength / authorization

- Goal: facts only; point to contact page; no hype.
- EN: “We focus on China heavy-truck parts export (SINOTRUK / SHACMAN / WEICHAI and related). For formal company documents, contact sales via WhatsApp/email on the site.”
- 中文：「我们侧重中国重卡配件出口（重汽/陕汽/潍柴等）。正式资质文件请通过网站 WhatsApp/邮箱联系销售。」
- `[待确认：允许对外出示的资质名称列表]`
